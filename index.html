                GENTLE SUPPORT

                Tips from students

                ✦

                Small, practical ways to make starting feel easier.

                ◷

                Try five minutes

                Start with a short session. You can decide what comes next after you begin.

                ▤

                Break it into small parts

                Turn a large assignment into a few steps you can do one at a time.

                ⌁

                Make distractions harder

                Silence your phone and move it out of reach while you work.

                ↻

                Shrink a plan that slipped

                Choose a smaller next step and make a fresh start without aiming for perfect.

                ♡

                Ask for help early

                A friend, family member, or teacher can help you get unstuck.

                Invite someone to study

                Copy a friendly, no-pressure message to share privately.
